    How it works in production

    A queue-based backend where every step can fail safely and pick up where it left off. Nothing is sent to a customer without the owner's approval.

    Step 1
    Website formValidated, rate-limited, duplicate-checked intake API
    Step 2
    Job queuePostgres jobs with locking, retries and stuck-job recovery
    Step 3
    AI analysisStrict JSON schema, validation and anti-hallucination rules
    Step 4
    Telegram cardOwner approves, edits or rejects from their phone
    Step 5
    DeliveryGmail via an outbox, or copy-and-send for SMS/Viber
    Step 6
    DashboardLead quality, reply speed and outcomes per business

    Prompt-injection defense

    Customer text is treated as untrusted data. Attempts to steer the AI are detected and sent to a human instead of answered.

    No made-up facts

    Replies may only use the business's own facts. Prices, figures or promises that aren't in them block the draft.

    Multi-tenant security

    Postgres row-level security and role checks: an owner can only ever read or act on their own business's leads.

    Exactly-once sending

    A transactional outbox and single-use approval tokens. A double tap or a crash never sends a reply twice.

    Crash recovery

    A sweeper retries stuck jobs, re-sends lost Telegram cards and expires stale approvals. Interrupted emails are never resent blindly.

    Made for the Philippines

    Understands Taglish, "po/opo" and short mobile messages, and falls back to copy-and-send for customers who prefer SMS or Viber.
