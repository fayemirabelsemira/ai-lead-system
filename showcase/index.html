    Architecture

    How it works in production

    A queue-based backend where each step can fail safely and pick up where it left off. Nothing reaches a customer without the owner's approval.

    1. Website formValidated, rate-limited, duplicate-checked intake API
    2. Job queuePostgres jobs with locking, retries and stuck-job recovery
    3. AI analysisStrict JSON schema, validation and anti-hallucination rules
    4. Telegram cardThe owner approves, edits or rejects from their phone
    5. DeliveryGmail through an outbox, or copy-and-send for SMS and Viber
    6. DashboardLead quality, reply speed and outcomes per business

    Prompt-injection defense

    Customer text is untrusted data. Attempts to steer the AI are detected and handed to a person.

    No made-up facts

    Replies may only use the business's own facts. Unknown prices, figures or promises block the draft.

    Multi-tenant security

    Row-level security and role checks: an owner can only read or act on their own leads.

    Exactly-once sending

    A transactional outbox and single-use approval tokens. A double tap or a crash never sends twice.

    Crash recovery

    A sweeper retries stuck jobs, re-sends lost cards and expires stale approvals. Interrupted emails are never resent blindly.

    Made for the Philippines

    Understands Taglish, "po/opo" and short mobile messages, with copy-and-send for SMS and Viber customers.
